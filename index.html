📚 What I Learned (DSKP Year 6 Maths 6.1)

  • Regular Polygons: All side lengths and interior angles are equal!
  • Interior Angles: Triangle = 60°, Square = 90°, Pentagon = 108°, Hexagon = 120°, Heptagon ≈ 128.6°, Octagon = 135°.
  • Angle Types: Acute (<90°), Right (90°), Obtuse (90° to 180°), Straight (180°).
  • Protractor Accuracy: Always align vertex at center point, line up 0° baseline, and pick inner or outer scale based on baseline ray.
