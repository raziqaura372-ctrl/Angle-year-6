# STUDENT EXPEDITION USER GUIDE
## Welcome Expedition Leader!

### Expedition Overview
Welcome to the **Desert Geometry Expedition: The Quest for the Hidden Oasis**! You have been chosen to lead an exploration team across an ancient desert civilization. To unlock ancient gates, construct water compasses, and repair temple structures, you must master the geometry of **ANGLES**.

---

### Key Expedition Tools
1. **Golden Ray Handle:** Drag the orange handle on the dynamic angle canvas to widen or narrow an angle.
2. **Virtual Protractor:** Click "Virtual Protractor" to overlay a transparent protractor. Snap it to vertex V to measure exact degrees!
3. **Polygon Grid Explorer:** Choose grid lines (Square or Isometric Triangular) and drag corner points (vertices) to draw shapes from 3 sides (Segi Tiga) up to 8 sides (Oktagon).
4. **DUNE AI Companion:** Need help? Click the floating DUNE icon at the bottom right for Socratic hints and clues!
5. **Badge Sanctuary:** Collect XP, Desert Coins, and Badges as you complete each mission!
